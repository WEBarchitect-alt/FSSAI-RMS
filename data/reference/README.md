# SENTRA-FS Regulatory Reference Library

This directory contains official regulatory and guidance references used by SENTRA-FS.

Rules:
1. Prefer official FDA sources.
2. Do not infer regulatory requirements from refusal records.
3. Do not create compliance rules until the underlying source is verified.
4. Every downloaded document must have a source entry in source_registry.csv.
5. Preserve original source documents where practical.
6. Record retrieval dates.
7. Distinguish regulations, guidance, reference material, and datasets.
8. Do not treat FDA Import Refusal records themselves as regulatory requirements.
9. Regulatory comparisons must cite the underlying official source.

Directory structure:

import_requirements/
    Requirements governing importation of food

import_alerts/
    FDA Import Alerts and DWPE references

compliance_guides/
    FDA Compliance Policy Guides and related compliance guidance

food_safety/
    Food safety requirements and guidance

food_labeling/
    Food labeling requirements and guidance

dietary_supplements/
    Dietary supplement requirements and guidance

regulations/
    Federal regulations / CFR references

metadata/
    Source metadata, mappings and reference indexes

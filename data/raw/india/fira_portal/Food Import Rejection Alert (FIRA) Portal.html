Welcome to FIRA

A Digital System for Exchange of information on food import rejections

  • Rapid exchange of information amongst authorities worldwide on food safety & health risks derived from rejected food;
  • Enables relevant food authorities/stakeholders to take immediate action for prevention & control of risk before it causes harm;
  • Online interactive interface for rapid dissemination of information to ensure enhanced traceability & transparency;
  • Serves as a valuable database for tracking rejected food products and further strengthening of the Risk Management System (RMS).

Legal Basis: Regulation 11(7) of the Foood Safety and Standards(Import) Regulation, 2017 empowers the Food Authority to issue Food alert notification.
